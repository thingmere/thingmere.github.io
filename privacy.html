Thingmere Upload (YouTube API Services)

Thingmere Upload is a private tool used only by the owner of the Thingmere YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services to upload the channel's own videos, to set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions and publication times, and to read the channel's own statistics.

  • What it reads: the channel's own list of videos with their titles, descriptions, tags, status, publication times, caption tracks and statistics.
  • What it stores: the access permission (OAuth token) and a log of its own changes (video ID, time and what was changed). Both stay on the owner's computer. It uses no cookies.
  • What it shares: nothing. It accesses no data of any other YouTube user or channel, and no data from YouTube is sold, shared with third parties or shown to anyone else.
  • By using Thingmere Upload, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. How Google handles data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • The permission can be revoked at any time in the Google account's security settings: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Publishing tool for Facebook and Instagram (Meta Platform)

The operator uses a private app on the Meta Platform only for the operator's own Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts. It publishes and schedules the channels' own videos and reads the statistics of these own posts.

  • What it reads: the operator's own Pages and Instagram accounts, their own posts and the statistics of these posts.
  • What it stores: the access permission (token) and a log of its own posts (post ID, time). Both stay on the operator's computer or in the operator's own cloud storage. It uses no cookies.
  • What it shares: nothing. It does not access, collect or store data of any other Facebook or Instagram user, and nothing is sold or passed on. How Meta handles data is described in the Meta Privacy Policy.

Data deletion

The tools described above store no data of other people. The only stored data are the operator's own access permissions and posting logs. To remove the app's access, open the Facebook settings under "Business integrations" (or the Google account's security settings for YouTube) and remove the app; the stored permission then stops working and is deleted from the operator's systems. If you believe any data about you is held, write to thingmere@gmail.com and it will be checked and deleted within 30 days.
